# Steinberg/Yamaha "Omnivocal Beta" in Cubase 15 — setup, usage and limits (state as of 2026-10-06)

Reading guide: "official" = Yamaha/Steinberg documents or staff; "forum" = user reports on forums (hearsay unless a staff name is given). Quotes were captured through an automated page reader, so short quotes are reliable in substance but should be spot-checked against the PDF before being reproduced verbatim. Target machine context: Windows 11, Cubase 15.0.30 (LE or AI licence bundled with a Kokko KB-25), `OmnivocalBeta.vst3` 1.2.5 (Yamaha Corporation), 2 voicebanks under `C:\ProgramData\Yamaha\OmnivocalBeta`.

## 1. What exactly is Omnivocal, who develops it, and how does it relate to VOCALOID / VOCALOID:AI / VOCALOID β-STUDIO (VX-β)?

### Takeaway
Omnivocal Beta is a Yamaha-developed singing-voice synthesis VST3 instrument that is deeply integrated into Cubase 15 (lyrics are typed on notes in the Key Editor) and is supported and documented by Yamaha, not Steinberg. Japanese press says it comes from Yamaha's VOCALOID team as a separate product line from VOCALOID; its workflow and parameter set match Yamaha's earlier VX-β prototype (VOCALOID:AI technology), but Yamaha has not publicly stated which engine it uses.

### Cited Findings
- Official Steinberg wording at launch (5 Nov 2025): "The beta Omnivocal vocal synthesis engine also adds a new world of expressive, humanlike singing voices. Using Yamaha's cutting-edge vocal synthesis, Omnivocal will sing notes and lyrics entered into the Key Editor." — [Steinberg press release, Cubase 15](https://ocl-steinberg-live.steinberg.net/_storage/asset/818311/storage/master/Press%20Release%20-%202025-11-05%20-%20Cubase%2015%20-%20EN.pdf)
- Official Yamaha product page (title: "The All-in-One Vocal Synth Plugin Now Available with Cubase"): "Fully integrated with Steinberg's Cubase. Create vocal tracks directly in the DAW—enter lyrics and melodies in the Key Editor without launching any external software."; format "VST3 (Instrument)"; supported DAWs "Cubase 15, Nuendo 15"; "Features two voicebanks of carefully selected voices to cover a wide range of genres." — [Yamaha Omnivocal page](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/)
- Yamaha's Japanese news release (6 Nov 2025) describes it as a singing-synthesis VST instrument bundled as standard: 「歌声合成の VST インストゥルメント「Omnivocal Beta」を標準搭載しました。」 — [Yamaha news release PDF](https://www.yamaha.com/ja/news_release/files/news/25110601/pdf/2511060101.pdf)

- DTM Station (6 Nov 2025, headline says a new plug-in "by the VOCALOID development team"): developed by Yamaha by applying the singing-synthesis technology cultivated with VOCALOID, while being a new plug-in distinct from VOCALOID itself; currently a beta, with a standalone release planned for the future. — [DTM Station](https://www.dtmstation.com/archives/74379.html)
- Another Japanese review (6 Nov 2025) says it is by the "VOCALOIDチーム", a "別ラインの新技術" (separate technology line), and that a standalone plug-in is planned ("今後単体プラグイン化も予定されている"). — [keiichi.blog](https://keiichi.blog/cubase15-vocaloid-vsti-ai-stem-separation/)

- Fan wiki: VX-β is "a prototype VST3/AU plug-in voice synthesis engine" and "was not considered a beta version of the next version of VOCALOID products". — [Vocaloid Wiki: VX-β](https://vocaloid.fandom.com/wiki/VX-%CE%B2)
- Community reading (hearsay): vocal-synth users describe Omnivocal as "Vocaloid 6 (or even vxb...) again, but in an even more 'professional vst' package", working "in the same real-time way as VXB", using ARPAbet phonemes, English and Japanese only ("no Chinese!"), with two voicebanks simply called Male and Female. — [VocaVerse thread, 7–8 Nov 2025](https://vocaverse.network/threads/omnivocal-by-yamaha.10333/)
- Community reading (hearsay): "a slightly better vocal synth than VX-β which comes packaged with Vocaloid 6". — [PluginGuru forum, 10 Nov 2025](https://forums.pluginguru.com/favorite-libraries/host-daw-cubase-15-pro-vocal-synthesis-plugin/)

- A forum user notes Steinberg/Yamaha market it as "vocal synthesis", and that the word "AI" does not appear in the Cubase 15 feature list or in the Omnivocal manual. — [Steinberg forum: Whats your take on Omnivocal?](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/whats-your-take-on-omnivocal/1015700)
- Sound On Sound calls it "a virtual vocalist instrument plug-in" built on Yamaha's vocal synthesis technology, positioned against Dreamtonics and ACE Studio. — [SOS, "Cubase 15: Exploring Omnivocal", John Walden, Feb 2026](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/cubase-15-exploring-omnivocal)
- KVR lists it as "Omnivocal by Yamaha", VST3, macOS/Windows, current version "1.2.5 beta", supported hosts Cubase and Nuendo. — [KVR product page](https://www.kvraudio.com/product/omnivocal-by-yamaha)

### Inferences
- Omnivocal is most plausibly a productised, simplified descendant of VX-β: same real-time-in-the-DAW concept, same Key Editor "Text"-field lyric entry in Cubase, and the same parameter names (Air, Formant, Attack, Vibrato, Power), but with only two anonymous voicebanks and without VX-β's internal sequencer/file import. This is an inference from matching features plus Japanese press and community statements, not an official Yamaha statement.
- Because VX-β is described officially as VOCALOID:AI technology, Omnivocal is probably a neural (AI) singing synthesizer in the same family, even though the marketing avoids the word "AI".
- For someone who mainly works in another DAW, VX-β (with a VOCALOID6 Editor licence) is the Yamaha product designed to run outside Cubase; Omnivocal is not.

### Gaps
- No official Yamaha/Steinberg statement was found that names the engine (VOCALOID:AI or otherwise) or explicitly links Omnivocal to VX-β.
- The real identities/names of the two voicebanks are not published; documentation only shows generic Female/Male.

## 4. What is the exact workflow in Cubase (track, notes, lyrics, phonemes, voices, parameters, MIDI CC, polyphony, live playing, export)?

### Takeaway
Load "Omnivocal Beta" on an instrument track (never the VSTi rack or a MIDI track), put one note per syllable in a MIDI part, select a note in the Key Editor and type words in the info line's "Text" field, then press Enter; Cubase/Omnivocal converts the text to bracketed ARPAbet-style phonemes and spreads a phrase across the following notes. Voices are just a female and a male singer with three styles, shaped by about a dozen knobs; playing a MIDI keyboard live only produces a sine guide tone, and the voice is heard on playback.

### Cited Findings

**Track setup**
- "Create an instrument track, not a MIDI track, and select MIDI Part in Event Type." Two documented ways: right zone → "Media" tab → "VST Instruments" → drag "Omnivocal Beta" into the track list; or click "+ (Add Track)" → "Instrument" → choose Omnivocal Beta. — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- "In Pattern Event mode, the plugin can be launched, but it cannot produce sound." — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- "Omnivocal Beta can only be used when inserted into an instrument track. For example, if you insert it into a rack instrument, it won't launch." Loading it in the VSTi rack shows "Omnivocal Beta Is Not Available in This DAW"; fix: remove it from the VSTi Rack (right zone), create an Instrument Track and select Omnivocal Beta as its instrument. — [Yamaha Omnivocal Beta FAQ](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_faq_en.pdf); [Steinberg Help Center article](https://www.steinberg.help/r/yamaha-omnivocal-beta-shows-an-error-omnivocal-beta-is-not-available-in-this-daw)

**Entering notes and lyrics**
- Lyrics go in the "Text" field on the info line of the Key Editor when a note is selected: select the first note, type the lyric (or the whole phrase), press Enter; "Press Enter to automatically assign the corresponding phonetic symbols to each note in order." Example: "What a fine day" on four notes becomes `[w ah t]`, `[ah]`, `[f ay n]`, `[d ey]`; 「ありがとお」 on five notes becomes `[a]`, `[r i]`, `[g a]`, `[t o]`, `[o]`. — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- If "Text" is not visible: it is at the far right of the info line and may be off-screen; right-click the Info Line → "Set up Info Line" and reduce the displayed items. — [Yamaha Omnivocal Beta FAQ](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_faq_en.pdf)
- Note-by-note keyboard flow (forum moderator Martin.Jirsak): click the Text field, type, press Enter, press Arrow Right to select the next note, repeat. Whole-phrase entry on the first note "works on Windows only so far – Cubase 15.0.5; there is a bug on Mac" (statement of 23 Nov 2025). — [Steinberg forum: Omnivocal -- how to enter lyrics](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omnivocal-how-to-enter-lyrics/1013591); [Steinberg forum: OMNIVOCAL Text Chart?](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omnivocal-text-chart/1015856)
- When redistributing a phrase, first delete the text from all the following notes and keep it only on the first note (forum). — [Steinberg forum: OMNIVOCAL Text Chart?](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omnivocal-text-chart/1015856)
- SOS: entering "broken heart" on the first note of a three-note phrase spreads it one syllable per note; after analysis "the phonetic form is also shown in the Text box and superimposed on the MIDI notes". — [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/cubase-15-exploring-omnivocal)
- Score Editor lyrics are not used: "Omnivocal is not using the Lyrics parameter as an input. Instead, it's using the Text parameter (from the Key Editor) as an input." (moderator Martin.Jirsak, 18 May 2026). The Text-based lyrics also do not survive MIDI file export/import, and there is no paste-a-whole-lyric-sheet function (forum, Nov 2025). — [Steinberg forum: Lyrics in the score editor are not used in Omnivocal Beta](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/lyrics-in-the-score-editor-are-not-used-in-omnivocal-beta/1032813); [Steinberg forum: Omnivocal -- how to enter lyrics](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omnivocal-how-to-enter-lyrics/1013591)
- Notes without text sing the "Default Lyric" chosen in the plug-in's Settings (gear icon): "Ahh" (`[aa]`), "La" (`[l a]`) or "Do Re Mi" ("the lyric is determined by mapping the note's pitch name to the scale degrees of C major"; sharps/flats treated as naturals). — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- One syllable per note: "You cannot assign multiple syllables to a single note. You can simulate this by splitting the note and assigning one syllable to each split note." — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- Forum troubleshooting: the number of notes must equal the number of syllables or the text piles onto the first note and the rest sing "Ah"; enter short chunks (about four words); delete accidentally duplicated notes (they cause garbled or accented pronunciation); unrecognised words may be read with a Japanese accent; moving the parts to a freshly created instrument track fixed a stuck case. — [Steinberg forum: Omnivocal beta is not "singing"?](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omnivocal-beta-is-not-singing/1022069)

**Special symbols (typed in the Text field)**
- Melisma: a hyphen gives `[-]`, which "lengthens the vowel of the syllable immediately before it". "Ama-zing" → `[ah] [m ey] [-] [z ih ng]`; "A-mazing" → `[ah] [-] [m ey] [z ih ng]`; two hyphens → two `[-]` notes. Japanese long-vowel mark ー also maps to `-`. — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- Breath: `[BR]` (uppercase) forces a breath, `[br]` (lowercase) is also a breath symbol. Silence: `[SIL]` (uppercase) forces silence; `[sil]` (lowercase) is treated as a rest with automatic breath insertion. Glottal stop: `[!]` re-articulates between vowels. Japanese sokuon っ: `[cl]`. Devoicing: "You can unvoice a vowel by entering '0' (half-width zero) in the lyrics." — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- Direct phoneme entry: enclose space-separated phonetic symbols in square brackets, e.g. `[f u]` for ふ. — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)

**Languages and phoneme set**
- Lyrics can be English or Japanese, detected automatically from the text, and can be mixed in one phrase (example 「さいこおのMusicで」). — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- English symbols (39, ARPAbet-style, with the manual's example words): aa (hot), ae (plan), ah (but), ao (long), aw (mouse), ay (try), b, ch (check), d, dh (these), eh (fresh), er (first), ey (make), f, g, hh (hair), ih (pitch), iy (speed), jh (joke), k, l, m, n, ng (king), ow (home), oy (voice), p, r, s, sh (shake), t, th (bath), uh (push), uw (spoon), v, w, y (year), z, zh (usual). — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- Japanese symbols are romanised kana (a i u e o, ka ki ku ke ko, sa shi su se so, ta chi tsu te to, na…, ha hi fu he ho, ma…, ya yu yo, ra…, wa, を = o, ん = N, plus palatalised kya/gya… and foreign-sound sets such as ti, tu, di, du, fa, fi, fe, fo, tsa, wi, we, wo). — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- No other languages: "It will either sound English, Japanese, or Omnivocal will sing 'la'" (forum, Nov 2025). Workaround used for French/Creole and suggested for others: spell words phonetically with the English symbols; openipa.org was recommended as a helper. — [Steinberg forum: Omnivocal languages](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omnivocal-languages/1014021); [Steinberg forum: Omnivocal experiences so far](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omnivocal-experiences-so-far/1019929)

**Voices and controls (manual v1.2.5.0)**
- Two voicebanks selected in the "Singer" dropdown (documented only as Female and Male) and a "Singing Style" dropdown: "Dynamic" ("larger pitch variations, producing an emotionally expressive singing voice"), "Straight" ("stable and natural pitch transitions, resulting in a smooth and natural singing voice"), "Clean" ("minimal pitch variation, producing a clear and natural singing voice"; added in 1.2.5). — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- "Identity" group: "Formant" (tone colour, 0–100 %, default 50), "Attack" (clarity of pronunciation onset, 0–100 %, default 50), "Air" (breathiness, 0–100 %, default 50), "Transpose" (−12 to +12 semitones), "Fine Tune" (−100 to +100 cents), "Pitchbend Range" (0–12, default 2). "Expression" group: "Power" (dynamics including volume and tone, 0–100 %, default 65), "Vibrato Auto" (depth of automatic vibrato, default 50 %), "Vibrato Manual" (depth of manually controlled vibrato, default 0 %). "Post Processing" group: "Presence" (0–100 %, default 10), "Output" (−12.0 to +12.0 dB, default 0.0). — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- Knob handling: drag up/down, click the value to type a number, double-click to reset to default; Pitchbend Range uses up/down buttons or a number click. Settings (gear icon) contains "Guide Tone" (on/off) and "Default Lyric". — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- MIDI CC: only three mappings exist — CC 1 → Vibrato Manual, CC 7 → Output, CC 11 → Power; "Parameters other than those listed above do not respond to MIDI Control Change (CC)." Latest input wins, and the manual warns against writing automation and sending CC to the same parameter simultaneously. — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- Automation: right-click the track → "Show Automation", pick the parameter in the automation lane, draw values or enable "W" and move the Omnivocal knobs while recording. Hardware control: via Cubase's MIDI Remote / Quick Controls. — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf); [Yamaha Omnivocal Beta FAQ](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_faq_en.pdf)
- Real-time behaviour, conflicting descriptions: Yamaha says "All simple, intuitive parameters respond in real time, even during playback in the DAW"; SOS (early beta) says only pitch-bend, Presence and Output act outside the synthesis engine in real time, while Formant, Attack, Air, Power and Vibrato make the engine re-render the vocal, which "happens automatically and very quickly in the background". — [Yamaha Omnivocal page](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/); [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/cubase-15-exploring-omnivocal)
- Velocity is not documented in the manual; a forum user reports MIDI note velocity affects each word's volume and that too-short notes hurt pronunciation (lengthen them). — [Steinberg forum: Omnivocal - First try with 1.2.5](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omnivocal-first-try-with-1-2-5/1028709)
- Transposition: track/MIDI-modifier transpose did not shift the voice correctly in Nov 2025 tests (original tone still audible); use the plug-in's own "Transpose" control (forum). — [Steinberg forum: Omni Vocal Feedback and Bugs](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omni-vocal-feedback-and-bugs/1015722)

**Live playing, harmonies, export**
- "Guide Tone": when on, "a guide tone is played when entering notes"; it "is for pitch confirmation only and cannot be played as output from the voicebank." — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- Live MIDI-keyboard input is recorded, but while playing you hear "just a sine-type sound"; on playback each note sings "ah" until lyrics are added (forum, Mar 2026). SOS likewise: "Omnivocal provides a simple synth sound for auditioning purposes as you play". — [Steinberg forum: Omnivocal note entry](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omnivocal-note-entry/1026428); [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/cubase-15-exploring-omnivocal)
- Harmonies/doubles are built with additional Omnivocal instrument tracks: duplicate the part, edit the notes (octave, third above, counter-melody), switch singer, and slightly randomise note starts; SOS's audio examples stack several male/female instances. — [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/cubase-15-exploring-omnivocal); [SOS audio examples](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/cubase-15-exploring-omnivocal-audio-examples)
- Solo quirk: "Omnivocal Beta is not muted when Solo Editor Mode is enabled on other tracks." — [Yamaha Omnivocal Beta FAQ](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_faq_en.pdf)
- Export to audio is not covered by the manual or FAQ. Forum evidence is mixed: rendered output was fine for some users (Nov 2025), while one user in May 2026 reported that export rendered only the synth (guide) sound and that Freeze/unfreeze or deactivate/activate corrupted words and syllables. — [Steinberg forum: Omnivocal in Cubase 15 is fantastic](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omnivocal-in-cubase-15-is-fantastic/1013396); [Steinberg forum: Quite some troubles with Omnivocals](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/quite-some-troubles-with-omnivocals/1032707)
- Cubase 15.0.21 fixed a general bug where "Audio Export and Render In Place could hang near completion in certain project configurations". — [Steinberg forum: Cubase 15.0.21 Maintenance Update](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/cubase-15-0-21-maintenance-update/1030073)

### Inferences
- Each Omnivocal instance is effectively a monophonic singer: one syllable per note, no documented chord handling, and overlapping/duplicate notes are reported to break pronunciation. Polyphony means more tracks, one instance per voice.
- It is not a live-performance instrument: the engine reads the notes and text of the MIDI part and synthesises ahead of/during playback, which is why a keyboard only triggers the guide tone.
- If pitch bend "does nothing" in an older project, check "Pitchbend Range": its default was 0 before version 1.2.5 and is 2 since.
- Safest bounce path given the mixed reports: turn "Guide Tone" off, play the passage once so it is synthesised, then use Render in Place or Export Audio Mixdown and audition the result; a real-time export is a reasonable fallback if an offline export yields only the guide tone. Rendering to audio and then removing or disabling the Omnivocal part also sidesteps the project-bloat and Freeze issues. This procedure is an inference, not documented.
- For Portuguese or other unsupported languages, the practical route is manual phoneme entry in brackets using the 39 English symbols (and Japanese vowels where they fit better), accepting an accent.

### Gaps
- No keyboard shortcut dedicated to lyric entry is documented; only Enter and Arrow Right are mentioned.
- Polyphony/overlap behaviour, pitch range, minimum note length and velocity response are not specified officially.
- No documentation on VST3 Note Expression support beyond the note "Text" attribute.
- No official export/bounce procedure; whether real-time export is required is unknown.
- Whether the plug-in UI or the Cubase "Text" field name is localised in Portuguese (Brazil) could not be verified: no Portuguese Cubase 15 web manual exists at the expected steinberg.help paths (English, German and Spanish do exist) and the Omnivocal manual exists only in English and Japanese. Portuguese UI terms therefore have to be read off the installed program.
- The article bodies of the Cubase LE/AI 15 web manual (Key Editor info line, instrument tracks) could not be read; only the page addresses were confirmed.

## 5. Does it work as a normal VST3 instrument in other hosts (REAPER, FL Studio)?

### Takeaway
No: every official source restricts it to Cubase 15 and Nuendo 15, and Steinberg states it depends on a special Cubase-specific connection that exists only on instrument tracks; even inside Cubase's own VSTi rack, and inside Steinberg's Dorico, it refuses to run with "Omnivocal Beta Is Not Available in This DAW". No first-hand report from REAPER or FL Studio was found, but the same refusal is the expected result.

### Cited Findings
- Supported DAWs: "Cubase 15, Nuendo 15". — [Yamaha Omnivocal page](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/)
- Steinberg Help Center (published 7 Nov 2025): Omnivocal's text-based vocal shaping requires "a specific connection to the VST application" and "works exclusively on Instrument Tracks and is not supported in the VSTi Rack"; the symptom is the message "Omnivocal Beta Is Not Available in This DAW". — [Steinberg Help Center article](https://helpcenter.steinberg.de/hc/en-us/articles/30879147782034-Yamaha-Omnivocal-Beta-shows-an-error-Omnivocal-Beta-Is-Not-Available-in-This-DAW)
- Steinberg's Daniel Spreadbury on Dorico (6 Nov 2025): "Omnivocal relies on special, Cubase-specific integration that is not possible with Dorico as things stand." — [Steinberg forum (Dorico): Omnivocal](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/omnivocal/1013638)
- Yamaha staff (10 Nov 2025): "Omnivocal can not work with Rack track. please stand Omnivocal from instrument track." — [Steinberg forum: Cubase 15: Installation problem with Omnivocal](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/cubase-15-installation-problem-with-omnivocal/1013424)
- The manual adds that it also needs an instrument track rather than a MIDI track, and makes no sound in Pattern Event mode. — [Omnivocal Beta User Manual v1.2.5.0](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/pdf/omnivocal_beta_user_manual_en.pdf)
- KVR lists supported hosts as Cubase and Nuendo only. — [KVR product page](https://www.kvraudio.com/product/omnivocal-by-yamaha)
- Yamaha support does not cover "Third-party integrations or DAW compatibility issues". — [Yamaha Omnivocal support policy](https://www.yamaha.com/en/about/business/music-connect/omnivocal/support-policy/)
- A standalone (not Cubase-bound) plug-in is reported as planned by Japanese press. — [DTM Station](https://www.dtmstation.com/archives/74379.html); [keiichi.blog](https://keiichi.blog/cubase15-vocaloid-vsti-ai-stem-separation/)
- Contrast: Yamaha's VX-β does run in other DAWs (VST3/AAX/AU) by loading sequence files into its own internal sequencer, though piano-roll lyric editing is Cubase-only. — [vocaloid.com: VX Beta](https://www.vocaloid.com/en/learn/ln6401/); [DTM Station, 2023](https://www.dtmstation.com/archives/62620.html)
- Background: the VST3 SDK defines a per-note "Note Expression Text event" with type `kTextTypeID` or `kPhoneticTypeID` carrying UTF-16 text for an individual note. — [VST3 SDK documentation: NoteExpressionTextEvent](https://steinbergmedia.github.io/vst3_doc/vstinterfaces/structSteinberg_1_1Vst_1_1NoteExpressionTextEvent.html)

### Inferences
- In REAPER or FL Studio the file `OmnivocalBeta.vst3` will probably be scanned and may open, but it should show the "not available in this DAW" message and not sing, because those hosts provide neither the Cubase connection nor any way to attach per-note lyric text. There is no documented way to enter lyrics outside Cubase/Nuendo.
- The integration is evidently more than standard per-note text events, since it fails even in Cubase's own rack and on MIDI tracks; the plug-in appears to need host access to the track's MIDI part (notes plus text) in advance.
- Practical route for a REAPER-centred workflow: write the melody anywhere, import it as MIDI into Cubase LE/AI 15, add lyrics there, render the vocal to WAV, and import the audio into REAPER. Lyrics will not travel inside the MIDI file in either direction.

### Gaps
- No first-hand test report of Omnivocal 1.2.5 in REAPER, FL Studio, Studio One, Ableton Live or Cubase 14 was found; the behaviour there (scan result, exact message, any sound) is unverified and would need a test on the target machine.
- The technical nature of the "specific connection" is not documented publicly.

## 7. What are the usage rights for the generated vocals?

### Takeaway

### Inferences
- Releasing songs with Omnivocal vocals on streaming services or selling them is permitted; covering or reusing someone else's copyrighted melody/MIDI still needs the normal rights, exactly as with any instrument.
- The remaining grey areas are the "services based on the software" restriction (for example selling Omnivocal vocal rendering as a service) and any future change of terms when the beta ends.
