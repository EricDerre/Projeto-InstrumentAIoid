Reading notes for the report writer (not a section): every price below was read on the cited page on 2026-10-06 unless another date is given; currency and tax status are stated next to each price. ## 1. VOCALOID (Yamaha): current version, what is included, price, trial, operating systems, plug-in formats

### Takeaway
VOCALOID6 is still the current generation (no VOCALOID7 on the official site); the editor is at Ver. 6.13.3 (17 Sep 2026), costs US$225 before tax with about 24–25 bundled voices plus Cubase AI, and has a 31-day full-feature trial. On Windows it needs Windows 11 22H2+, a 4th-gen-or-later Intel Core CPU and 8 GB RAM, and runs standalone or as VST3 with ARA2; the cheapest entry is a voicebank that ships with the free 2-track "VOCALOID6 Editor Lite" (for example Hatsune Miku V6 at US$78).

### Inferences
- "25 voices" on the buy page is most likely 21 VOCALOID6 AI voicebanks (20 named as of v6.9 plus one added later) + the 4 VOCALOID5 voices; the bundled count has grown from 16 (Dec 2024) to 20–21 (2026) at the same US$225 price.
- For a DAW user (REAPER, Cubase, etc.) only the full editor matters as a plug-in: Editor Lite is standalone-only, so the workflow with a Lite-bundled voicebank is "write vocals standalone, export WAV, import into the DAW".
- VOCALOID6 is the most demanding of the mainstream editors on the Windows side (Windows 11 22H2+, 8 GB RAM, 25 GB disk); a Windows 10 or low-RAM machine is outside the official spec.
- With a Lite-bundled voicebank at US$78–92, the real entry price for VOCALOID is now far below the US$225 headline price.

## 3. Synthesizer V Studio (Dreamtonics): Studio 2 Pro vs the free tier, AI voices, prices; Kasane Teto's voicebank; other star voices

### Takeaway
Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro (released 21 March 2025) is a US$99 one-time purchase with no subscription, voices at about US$79–100 each, and a 14-day trial that afterwards "reverts to limited functionality" (the free mode: one track, no AI retakes or vocal modes). The latest stable build is 2.2.1 (2 March 2026); the 2.3.0 Technical Preview of 22 May 2026 adds experimental Brazilian Portuguese, French and German, but no stable 2.3 had shipped by 1 October 2026.

## 8. Which engines and voices can sing in Portuguese, English and Japanese (cross-lingual support)

### Takeaway
Japanese and English are covered by every major engine, but Portuguese is not: among commercial products only ACE Studio lists Portuguese as a released singing language, and Synthesizer V has experimental Brazilian Portuguese solely in the 2.3.0 Technical Preview (May 2026), still without a stable release in early October 2026. VOCALOID6, VoiSona, CeVIO AI, Piapro and Emvoice have no Portuguese; the free OpenUtau has a Brazilian Portuguese phonemizer for community voicebanks.

### Cited Findings
- VOCALOID6 AI voicebanks mix Japanese, English and Chinese in one voicebank; no Portuguese is listed — [vocaloid.com VOCALOID6](https://www.vocaloid.com/en/vocaloid6/); the wider compatible library adds only Spanish and Korean — [Wikipedia: Vocaloid 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocaloid_6)
- Hatsune Miku V6: Japanese and English, Chinese by future update — [SONICWIRE](https://sonicwire.com/product/virtualsinger/special/miku-v6-starter); Crypton's V4X/V3 products have separate English libraries — [SONICWIRE Miku V4X](https://sonicwire.com/product/virtualsinger/special/mikuv4x); [Crypton catalogue](https://ec.crypton.co.jp/pages/prod/virtualsinger)
- The Spanish VOCALOID3 voice MAIKA (Voctro Labs, 2013) carries extra phonemes for Catalan; the wiki page read does not document Portuguese — [Vocaloid Wiki: MAIKA](https://vocaloid.fandom.com/wiki/MAIKA)
- Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro (stable): cross-lingual singing across six languages — English, Japanese, Mandarin, Cantonese, Spanish, Korean — [Dreamtonics Synthesizer V](https://dreamtonics.com/synthesizerv/)
- Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro 2.3.0 Technical Preview (22 May 2026): experimental French, German and Brazilian Portuguese ("a relatively neutral Brazilian Portuguese accent") for updated Dreamtonics v2 voices — [Dreamtonics forum](https://forum.dreamtonics.com/t/synthesizer-v-studio-2-pro-2-3-0-technical-preview/8046); still preview-only as of 1 October 2026 according to forum users — [Dreamtonics forum thread, Oct 2026](https://forum.dreamtonics.com/t/how-long-does-dreamitonics-take-to-release-a-new-beta-update-or-final-release-for-2-3-0-update-part-2/8609)
- Kasane Teto (Synthesizer V 2): Japanese recording, cross-lingual English, Chinese, Cantonese, Spanish, Korean; Portuguese not listed — [AHS](https://www.ah-soft.com/synth-v/teto2/)
- ACE Studio 2.0: English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Italian, French and Portuguese — [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/news/ace-studio-20-timedomain)
- VoiSona: Japanese, English and (since 30 April 2025) Mandarin — [CeVIO & VoiSona Wiki: VoiSona](https://cevio.fandom.com/wiki/VoiSona); the free "Chis-A [tʃíːseɪ]" 2.0 sings all three cross-lingually — [Forest Watch (Impress)](https://forest.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2089046.html); English lyrics can be typed directly — [VoiSona Song](https://voisona.com/song/)
- CeVIO-family engines are described as primarily Japanese with limited English — [VI-Control vocal synth reference thread](https://vi-control.net/community/threads/reference-thread-an-exhausting-list-of-vocal-synth-engines.147683/)
- Emvoice One: English only — [KVR Audio product page](https://www.kvraudio.com/product/emvoice-one-by-emvoice)
- OpenUtau (free) ships a "PT-BR CVC" Brazilian Portuguese phonemizer (BRAPA notation, built-in G2P dictionary, "Neutral" accent), alongside English, Japanese and Spanish phonemizers — [OpenUtau wiki: Phonemizers](https://github.com/stakira/OpenUtau/wiki/Phonemizers)
- Fan ports of Adachi Rei for UTAU/DiffSinger are listed with many languages including Portuguese — [Vocaloid Wiki: Adachi Rei](https://vocaloid.fandom.com/wiki/Adachi_Rei)

### Inferences
- A Brazilian hobbyist who wants Portuguese lyrics today has three realistic routes: ACE Studio (released, paid, generic voices), Synthesizer V's 2.3.0 technical preview (experimental, Dreamtonics voices), or OpenUtau with a PT-BR voicebank (free, lower realism).
- With the famous character voices (Miku, Teto, GUMI, KAFU), Portuguese would have to be approximated with Japanese, English or Spanish phonemes; Teto's Spanish cross-lingual mode in Synthesizer V is probably the closest officially supported approximation.
- English is well served everywhere except the Piapro NT line and most CeVIO/VoiSona character libraries, which are Japanese-first.
